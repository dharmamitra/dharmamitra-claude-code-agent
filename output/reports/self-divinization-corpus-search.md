## Observations

- The strongest thread is terminological: Skt. **devatāhaṃkāra / ahaṃkāra / abhimāna** (nos. 4, 6, 22, 23) corresponds to Tib. **lha'i nga rgyal** (nos. 10, 11, 26) and to Chinese formulas such as **我身即是金剛薩埵** and **諦觀我此身即是執金剛** (nos. 14–16).
- 入我我入 as a fixed phrase is attested verbatim in this corpus only in T1798 (no. 18). 即身成佛 is abundant, but mostly in Japanese-transmitted or apocryphal esoteric texts and Tendai/Lotus commentaries (nos. 19–21).
- The Vajraḍākottara's "sidhyatīhaiva janmani" (no. 7) is a good Indic anchor for 即身成佛.
- The best "why it is valid" passages are Alakakalaśa (no. 22, divine pride to cancel ordinary ego), Bhavabhaṭṭa (no. 23, the body already is the deity), and the sdom gsum ṭīkā (no. 27, gnosis rather than the conventional self is the deity).
- On the critical side, the Brahmā formula of DN 1 runs through Abhidharma (no. 35) and Satyasiddhi (no. 36), which treat self-deification as a cognitive error rather than a metaphysical claim to refute.
