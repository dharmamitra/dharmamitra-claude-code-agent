## 1. Classification

**School:** Pramāṇa (Dignāga/Dharmakīrti)

**Opponent:** Nyāya-Vaiśeṣika — the pūrvapakṣa asserts that a nitya (permanent) entity can possess sāmarthya (causal capacity), echoing the Nyāya defence of permanent universals and substances with causal power. The sāmarthya + nitya framing is more characteristic of Nyāya-Vaiśeṣika padārtha theory than Mīmāṃsā, though the latter cannot be ruled out.

**Topic:** Ontological/epistemological (mixed) — the specific issue is the identity of sat (real existent) with arthakriyāśakti (causal efficacy) as the criterion of existence, combined with the kṣaṇabhaṅga (momentariness) thesis and its proof by anvayavyatireka.

**Register:** Bhāṣya prose; pūrvapakṣa–uttarapakṣa dialectical structure typical of Dharmakīrtian commentary. No metrical pattern detectable.

**Possible identity:** Dharmakīrti, Pramāṇavārttika (II.3 paraphrase) or Hetubindu; possibly a sub-commentary (ṭīkā) on one of these. Specific locus unknown.

**Technical terms attested:** sat, arthakriyāsamartha, kṣaṇika, sthira, vastu, sattvam, arthakriyāśakti, pūrvapakṣaḥ, nitya, sāmarthya, kṣaṇabhaṅga

## 3. Reconstructed Folio

```
yat sat tat ⟨kṣaṇikam eva⟩ [◆] arthakriyāsamartham |
tac ca kṣaṇikam iti ⟨anvayavyatirekeṇa⟩ [◇] siddham |
na hi sthiraṃ vastu ⟨arthakriyāṃ⟩ [◆] kartuṃ śaknoti |
ataḥ ⟨kṣaṇikasya⟩ [◇] sattvam arthakriyāśaktir eva |
pūrvapakṣaḥ: nityam api ⟨sahakāribhiḥ⟩ [◇] sāmarthyaṃ bhavet iti cet |
na, kṣaṇabhaṅge ⟨avastutva-prasaṅgaḥ⟩ [◆] siddhaḥ |
```

◆ Attested: lacunae 1, 3, 6 &nbsp;|&nbsp; ◇ Probable: lacunae 2, 4, 5 &nbsp;|&nbsp; ○ Speculative: none

---

## 4. Translation

Whatever is existent is necessarily [momentary], for it possesses the capacity for causal efficacy (*arthakriyāśakti*, causal efficiency). That it is momentary is established [through positive and negative concomitance (*anvayavyatirekeṇa*)]; for a permanent entity cannot perform [causal efficacy (*arthakriyā*)]. Therefore, for [the momentary], existence is precisely the capacity for causal efficacy.

**Opponent:** But might a permanent entity also possess causal capacity [through accessory conditions (*sahakāribhiḥ*)]?

**Response:** No, because in the case of momentary destruction, [the *reductio ad absurdum* of non-existence (*avastutva-prasaṅgaḥ*)] is established — that is, if a permanent entity were to require external conditions to act, it would forfeit its status as a real, independent entity.

## 5. Lacuna Notes

### Lacuna 1
**Position:** Line 1, between `tat` and `arthakriyāsamartham`
**Estimated akṣaras:** 4–5
**Syntactic slot:** Predicate adjective modifying `yat … tat`; exclusive identification marker
**Candidates:**
- ⟨kṣaṇikam eva⟩ — formulaic biconditional from Arcaṭa SA_T11_arhebt_u:1295, :4352: "yat sat tat kṣaṇikam eva / avadhāreṇa vyāptim āha"
- ⟨kṣaṇikam⟩ — without avadhāraṇa particle
**Chosen:** ⟨kṣaṇikam eva⟩ [◆ Attested]
**Rationale:** The `eva` is structurally required as avadhāraṇa marking the exclusive identification on which the subsequent syllogism depends. Arcaṭa provides the formula verbatim in at least three segments.
**Validation:** SA_T11_arhebt_u:1295 — direct attestation; SA_T11_arhebt_u:4352 — "yat sat tat kṣaṇikam eveti." Label: ◆

---

### Lacuna 2
**Position:** Line 2, between `kṣaṇikam iti` and `siddham`
**Estimated akṣaras:** 5–8
**Syntactic slot:** Instrumental of means modifying `siddham` — the epistemic warrant by which kṣaṇikatva is established
**Candidates:**
- ⟨anvayavyatirekeṇa⟩ — "through positive and negative concomitance"
- ⟨vyāptitaḥ⟩ — "by virtue of vyāpti" (shorter)
- ⟨vyāptiniścayāt⟩ — attested in Jayanta's paraphrase
**Chosen:** ⟨anvayavyatirekeṇa⟩ [◇ Probable]
**Rationale:** Arcaṭa (SA_T11_arhebt_u:4352) uses "anvayavyatirekarūpavyāptisiddhiḥ" in direct commentary on this thesis. Jayanta confirms the epistemic-warrant slot with "vyāptiniścayāt siddham etat." The instrumental fits bhāṣya register and the akṣara span.
**Validation:** SA_T11_arhebt_u:4352 — overlapping passage with same technical formula. Label: ◇

### Lacuna 3
**Position:** Line 3, between `vastu` and `kartuṃ śaknoti`
**Estimated akṣaras:** 4 (+ anusvāra)
**Syntactic slot:** Direct object of `kartuṃ śaknoti` — what the permanent entity cannot perform
**Candidates:**
- ⟨arthakriyāṃ⟩ — "causal efficacy"
- ⟨kāryāṇi⟩ — "effects"
**Chosen:** ⟨arthakriyāṃ⟩ [◆ Attested]
**Rationale:** `arthakriyā` is the thematic keyword throughout. SA_MB_mrgendraHK:2022 — "nityo bhāvaḥ … arthakriyāṃ kuryāt" provides the exact object-verb construction in the same dialectical context. Karṇakagomin (SA_T11_kgpramv:18591) — "arthakriyāsāmarthyam akṣaṇikasya na sambhavati."
**Validation:** SA_MB_mrgendraHK:2022 — direct attestation of `arthakriyāṃ kartuṃ` frame. Label: ◆

### Lacuna 4
**Position:** Line 4, between `ataḥ` and `sattvam`
**Estimated akṣaras:** 4
**Syntactic slot:** Genitive qualifying `sattvam` — "the existence of ___"
**Candidates:**
- ⟨kṣaṇikasya⟩ — "of the momentary"
- ⟨vastunaḥ⟩ — "of the entity"
**Chosen:** ⟨kṣaṇikasya⟩ [◇ Probable]
**Rationale:** The positive conclusion following line 3's negative proof: it is specifically the momentary whose existence just is arthakriyāśakti. Hetubindu (SA_T11_dhkhetuu:579) — "śaktir hi bhāva-lakṣaṇam." SA_T11_jnaniva:2126 uses `kṣaṇikasya` as genitive in a parallel argumentative position.
**Validation:** No verbatim attestation of the full phrase; follows attested Dharmakīrtian formulations closely. Label: ◇

### Lacuna 5
**Position:** Line 5 (pūrvapakṣa), between `nityam api` and `sāmarthyaṃ bhavet iti cet`
**Estimated akṣaras:** 5–7
**Syntactic slot:** Instrumental of means — the condition under which even a permanent entity might possess causal capacity
**Candidates:**
- ⟨sahakāribhiḥ⟩ — "through accessory conditions"
- ⟨sahakārisamāgame⟩ — "at the conjunction of auxiliaries" (locative)
**Chosen:** ⟨sahakāribhiḥ⟩ [◇ Probable]
**Rationale:** The standard Nyāya-Vaiśeṣika objection to kṣaṇabhaṅga invokes sahakārin (accessory conditions): even a permanent substance can produce sequential effects through sequential auxiliaries. Tattvasaṃgraha (SA_T04_tat:314) — "athāpi santi nityasya kramiṇaḥ sahakāriṇaḥ." SA_T04_tatpa:3732 confirms the pūrvapakṣa formula.
**Validation:** SA_T04_tatpa:3732 — parallel pūrvapakṣa with `nityasya … sahakāriṇaḥ` in the same argumentative position. Label: ◇

---

### Lacuna 6
**Position:** Line 6 (uttarapakṣa), between `kṣaṇabhaṅge` and `siddhaḥ`
**Estimated akṣaras:** 7–9
**Syntactic slot:** Masculine nominative singular, subject of `siddhaḥ` — what kṣaṇabhaṅga establishes as the reductio against the pūrvapakṣa
**Candidates:**
- ⟨avastutva-prasaṅgaḥ⟩ — "the reductio to non-entity-hood"
- ⟨arthakriyāvirodhaprasaṅgaḥ⟩ — "the reductio of arthakriyā-incompatibility" (too long)
**Chosen:** ⟨avastutva-prasaṅgaḥ⟩ [◆ Attested]
**Rationale:** Hetubindu (SA_T11_dhkhetuu:579) — "na, akṣaṇikatve **avastutva-prasaṅgāt**" gives the term verbatim as the Buddhist refutation label. The masculine nominative `avastutva-prasaṅgaḥ` agrees grammatically with `siddhaḥ`.
**Validation:** SA_T11_dhkhetuu:579 — direct attestation in the Hetubindu in the `na … prasaṅgāt` refutation structure, the likely source or sibling text of this folio. Label: ◆
